# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome different backgrounds, perspectives, and levels of experience.
- Respect project maintainers, contributors, and community spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers at `conduct@frontal.dev`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential where possible.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
community channels, events, and when someone represents the project in public.
Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions,
restrict participation, or remove access when behavior violates this Code.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented where possible.

Do not use OCP examples or integrations to exfiltrate credentials, bypass
authorization, or execute destructive actions without explicit approval.

This document is based on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
